Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At PulseFetch, accessible from https://pulsefetch.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by PulseFetch and how we use it in strict accordance with global privacy principles and advertising standards.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

PulseFetch operates as a client-side utility and lightweight edge-resolver. We do NOT require user account creation, registration, passwords, phone numbers, or payment credentials. We do NOT permanently store the videos, photos, or media content you fetch using our tool.

2. Log Files & Edge Analytics

Like many web services, PulseFetch follows a standard procedure of utilizing log files for rate-limiting, edge telemetry, and abuse mitigation via Cloudflare Workers. These logs include non-personally identifiable information such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. IP addresses are redacted or hashed after real-time rate validation and are never paired with personal identities.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

PulseFetch displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to pulsefetch.com and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Third-Party Links & Disclaimer

Our service provides metadata links directly pointing to official content hosting servers of supported social networks (such as Instagram or Facebook). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you are granted rights regarding your personal data. Because PulseFetch does not maintain user profiles, personal accounts, or email lists, we store zero personal datasets. If you have questions regarding edge logging or wish to exercise any legal rights, contact us at privacy@pulsefetch.com.

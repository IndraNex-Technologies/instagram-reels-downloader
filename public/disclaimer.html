PulseFetch Intellectual Property Stance

PulseFetch operates strictly as an independent search indexer and edge metadata parser. We do not host, store, replicate, or broadcast media files on our infrastructure. All video content, audio tracks, and images belong entirely to their respective authors and copyright owners.

1. No Media Storage Policy

All download links generated on PulseFetch point directly to original media URLs delivered by the originating content distribution networks (CDNs) of Instagram or Facebook. When a user requests a file, their browser or client retrieves the file directly from the third-party provider's edge.

2. Fair Use & Educational Exemption

PulseFetch is offered solely for personal, non-commercial, and fair-use archiving purposes (such as offline study, personal backups of creator-owned work, or quotation). Users bear sole legal responsibility for any subsequent distribution or use of files obtained via this utility.

3. DMCA Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner or an agent authorized to act on behalf of one, and you wish to block specific URL patterns or report an issue, please send an inquiry to our DMCA compliance desk at dmca@pulsefetch.com with:

  • Identification of the copyrighted material claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URLs on Instagram or Facebook.
  • Your contact email, physical address, and telephone number.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.

We process all legitimate requests within 24 to 48 business hours by updating our edge filters to reject resolution for specified links or accounts.
